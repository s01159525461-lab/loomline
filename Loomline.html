What you can do

Text and image layers

Several per side, each with its own size, rotation, opacity, shadow and outline.

Image tools

Crop, stretch, flip, brightness, contrast, hue and white background removal.

Five animations

Still, turntable, wind, walking and a knit-in reveal, with adjustable speed.

Image and video export

A PNG of the current view and a five second clip, where your browser supports recording.

Arabic and English

Interface and fonts for both languages, including right-to-left layout.

Private by design

Artwork is read and drawn in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every garment and animation is free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the studio and start designing.

Is my artwork uploaded anywhere?

No. Images are read and drawn locally in your browser.

What can I export?

A PNG of the current view and a five second video clip, depending on what your browser supports.

Can I use Arabic text?

Yes. Two Arabic fonts are included and the interface switches to right-to-left.
